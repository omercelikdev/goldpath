# Upgrading to 0.1.0-preview.3 (train accumulating — not yet released)

## Behavioral break: admin surfaces are tenant-scoped (admin-contract revision R1)

On apps with `multiTenancy: true`, the `/goldpath/admin/*` surfaces now scope every read
and verb to the AMBIENT tenant (same resolution as your business endpoints). What changes
for you:

- Operators who previously relied on `?tenant=` (or the implicit all-tenants view) need
  the new `goldpath-ops-all-tenants` role (configurable: `Goldpath:Auth:OpsAllTenantsRole`)
  on top of `goldpath-ops`. Without it: `403` on foreign tenants, ambient scope otherwise.
- Requests that reach the admin head WITHOUT tenant resolution now answer `400` instead
  of returning every tenant's rows (fail-closed).
- The campaign surface demands the cross-tenant role outright on multi-tenant apps (its
  rows carry no tenant column — it is cross-tenant by nature).
- Single-tenant apps (`multiTenancy: false`): no change.

Routes, nouns, envelopes and paging are unchanged — console/scripts keep working once the
operator carries the right role.

## Archival: erased entries stay chain-verifiable (audit A2)

`GoldpathArchiveEntry` gains `PreErasureContentHash` (one nullable column — add a
migration with `goldpath db add`): erasure now retains the SEALED content hash before
re-stamping, and chain verification never skips erased rows. Rows erased BEFORE this
train carry no retained hash and verification reports them ("no pre-erasure content
hash") — expected once per legacy erased row; document them against your erasure
evidence and move on.

Written limit: tamper of the REDACTED document after a lawful erasure is only provable
with an erasure-event-in-chain design — deferred with its trigger in the archival RFC.

## Behavioral break: two fail-open defaults are now fail-closed (audit A3)

- **OpenId without an `Audience` refuses to start.** Previously audience validation was
  silently disabled. Set `Goldpath:Auth:Audience`, or opt out EXPLICITLY with
  `Goldpath:Auth:AllowAnyAudience=true` (accepts any token the authority minted — the
  startup message spells this out).
- **SMTP is secure by default.** `UseSsl` now defaults to `true` (STARTTLS). A dev relay
  (MailHog etc.) needs `UseSsl=false` AND `AllowInsecureTransport=true` — credentials
  never travel plaintext by accident.
- Why runtime guards and not `GP####` rules: both are CONFIGURATION decisions, invisible
  to Roslyn — the ADR-0005 verifier here is the fail-fast itself.

## Fixed: `Strategy=None` + guarded admin route answered 500 (audit A4)

The None path now registers the ops policies and a deny-only authentication scheme, and
wires the pipeline: a guarded admin route answers an honest **401/403** instead of
crashing with "no authenticationScheme was specified". Open endpoints stay open — the
internal-service posture is unchanged.
